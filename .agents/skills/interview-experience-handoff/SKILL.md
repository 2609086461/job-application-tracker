# Interview Experience Handoff

Read [review-writing-rules.md](references/review-writing-rules.md) before creating or changing a review. Read [configuration.md](references/configuration.md) when locating or initializing the user's Feishu review library.

## Workflow

1. Confirm company, position, interview round and date. Ask when the recording or message does not establish them.
2. Find the company overview, preparation document, earlier-round reviews and the review-index record. Do not substitute recruitment-mail archives for interview materials.
3. When audio or video is attached, use the audio-transcription skill first and preserve the raw transcript as evidence.
4. Produce a draft that distinguishes what was explicitly recorded, what was reconstructed from context and what is supplemental explanation.
5. Creating or editing Feishu documents, index rows or attachments requires the user's explicit authorization. “整理并归档” is sufficient authorization for that named interview only; “先给草稿” is not.
6. Update the existing record for the same company, position, round and nearby date. Create a new document for a new round; never overwrite the previous round.
7. Re-read the document and index after writes. Verify headings, links, status and that the previous body was replaced rather than duplicated.

When the library is unavailable, say so. Do not invent document links, table tokens or project facts.
